Our Lady of Joy Homilies

Our Lady of Joy Roman Catholic Church, Carefree, AZ

Weekly homilies, recorded at Mass and published as a podcast.

Listen

The podcast feed for this page is feed.xml. Once the feed has its first episode and has been submitted in Spotify for Podcasters, this page will link to the show on Spotify and the other podcast apps.

How this page works

This is a GitHub Pages site for the oloj-homily-automation project. It exists mainly for machines, not visitors: podcast apps and Spotify read feed.xml here, and each episode's MP3 lives under audio/ at a public HTTPS address. The homily blog posts and transcripts are on the parish website.
